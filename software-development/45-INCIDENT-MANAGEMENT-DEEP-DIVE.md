## What is Incident Management?

### Definition

**Incident Management**: Process of managing and resolving system incidents.

**Key Concepts:**
- **Incident**: System incident
- **Response**: Incident response
- **Resolution**: Incident resolution
- **Prevention**: Incident prevention

### Real-World Analogy

**Incident Management = Emergency Response:**
- **Emergency**: System incident
- **Response team**: Incident response team
- **Resolution**: Emergency resolution
- **Prevention**: Emergency prevention

**IT Systems:**
- **Incident**: System failure
- **Response**: Incident response
- **Resolution**: Fix and restore
- **Prevention**: Prevent future incidents

Incident management is essential for system reliability and business continuity. Understanding incident lifecycle, classification, response, communication, post-incident review, and best practices is crucial for effective incident management.

- **Incident response**: Triage, investigation, mitigation, resolution
- **Incident communication**: Internal, external, executive communication
- **Post-incident review**: Timeline, root cause, impact, actions (incident report, action items, improvements)
