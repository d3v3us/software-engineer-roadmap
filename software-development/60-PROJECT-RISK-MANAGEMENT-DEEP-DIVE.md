## What is Project Risk Management?

- **Risk mitigation**: Mitigate risks

## Assessing Risks

### Risk Assessment Matrix

**Probability x Impact:**
```
        Low Impact    Medium Impact    High Impact
High     Medium        High            Critical
Medium   Low           Medium          High
Low      Low           Low             Medium
```

### Risk Scoring

**Risk Score:**
```
Risk Score = Probability × Impact

Probability: 1-5 (1=low, 5=high)
Impact: 1-5 (1=low, 5=high)
Risk Score: 1-25
```

**Risk Levels:**
- **Critical (20-25)**: Immediate action required
- **High (15-19)**: Action required soon
- **Medium (10-14)**: Monitor and plan
- **Low (5-9)**: Accept or monitor
- **Very Low (1-4)**: Accept

### Risk Assessment Example

**Example:**
```
Risk: New technology adoption
Probability: 4 (High - new technology)
Impact: 5 (High - project failure)
Risk Score: 20 (Critical)

Mitigation: 
- Proof of concept
- Training
- Expert consultation
```

## Mitigating Risks

### Mitigation Strategies

**1. Avoid:**
- **Strategy**: Eliminate risk
- **Example**: Use proven technology
- **When**: High impact, avoidable

**2. Mitigate:**
- **Strategy**: Reduce probability or impact
- **Example**: Training, proof of concept
- **When**: Can be reduced

**3. Transfer:**
- **Strategy**: Transfer to third party
- **Example**: Insurance, outsourcing
- **When**: Can be transferred

**4. Accept:**
- **Strategy**: Accept risk
- **Example**: Low impact risks
- **When**: Low impact, acceptable

### Mitigation Plans

**Plan Structure:**
```
Risk: [Description]
Probability: [Level]
Impact: [Level]
Risk Score: [Score]
Mitigation Strategy: [Strategy]
Action Items:
  - [Action 1]
  - [Action 2]
Owner: [Person]
Timeline: [Date]
Status: [Status]
```
